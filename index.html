Privacy Policy for Lys

Last updated: July 2026

At Lys, we deeply value the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the application. By using our app, you agree to the practices described in this document.

1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

Lys is a one-time purchase application with 100% local processing.

We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information, identifying data, or usage statistics to external servers.

2. Device Permissions (Camera and Storage)

To provide its core photography and editing features, the application requires the following permissions:

  • Camera: This permission is used exclusively to capture photos in real time within the application and to enable the use of visual filters.
  • Storage / Gallery: This permission is used only to allow you to save your edited photos directly to your device or to open existing photos (if supported by the app).

3. Local Processing and Storage

All image processing and filter application take place entirely on the user's device.

Your photos are never uploaded or sent to any external server.

Under no circumstances do we have access to the images you capture or edit using the application.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Our application contains no advertising of any kind and does not include any third-party SDKs (Software Development Kits) for analytics, tracking, marketing, or data monetization.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to technical changes or legal requirements imposed by app stores. Any updates will be published on this page.
